# Periapsis Zero: the story

The plot the Godot game tells, kept here so every scene's lines agree. 3091,
Earth-Moon space: two hundred million people, and everything they need
moves along convoy lanes.

## The people

- **You, "Kestrel".** An escort pilot named by your ship. One fighter, one
  licence, debts you don't talk about. Hired for the Aster on Mara's word.
- **Captain Elias Hale.** The Aster's captain. Honest, dry, careful: he filed
  a false flight plan and kept the real route on his own console. Flew on
  Mara's wing for eleven years. Dies in the ambush mid-sentence: "They knew
  our route. Kestrel, they knew exactly wh—"
- **Renn Ayers.** The Aster's engineer: warm, joking, the one who "patches
  the holes". She sold the route to Rook to clear a debt, believing the
  Hollow would take cargo, not lives. She leaves her post before the attack
  ("aft relays, two minutes") and launches a pod before the torpedoes hit.
- **Mara Voss.** Hearth's dockmaster, Hale's oldest friend. Sharp, unsentimental,
  generous in deeds rather than words. She got you the job and feels it.
  Your ally, and she will use you to get a name.
- **Governor of Hearth.** Never met yet; their seal is on Rook's permits.
- **Rook.** Hearth's moneylender. Never seen: a voice on an encrypted
  channel, amused and patient "until he isn't". Paid ₡40,000 for your
  recovery so that he owns you, and so that he could get to your recorder.
  Behind the Hollow (revealed in Act Two).
- **Hearth Traffic.** The station's bored, sardonic traffic controller.
- **Tamsin.** Captain of the Meridian, the convoy ship astern of the Aster.
  Dies with the convoy.
- **The Hollow.** Raiders with no transponders, no demands, no survivors.

## Prologue: the Aster

1. **Film.** The lanes, the Hollow, you, the Aster's job: vaccine for an
   outbreak at Tycho, six ships, a route nobody else knows.
2. **Corridor.** Hale on the PA. Renn introduces herself; she has "the
   long-range set open tonight" (it is how she will send the route).
3. **Bridge.** Hale briefs you; Mara vouched for you. The false flight plan:
   "Nobody, Renn." Renn: "...Nobody. Right."
4. **Flight deck.** Renn: "Whatever happens out there, it's not on you."
5. **Flight.** Escort. Renn is "off the board". The Hollow come out of the
   sun. Tamsin sees a pod away from the Aster's aft before the torpedoes.
   The Aster dies. Renn calls from her pod. A second blast takes the Kestrel.

## Act One: Hearth

6. **Wake.** Nine days later, Hearth's medbay. Mara: you were the only one
   pulled out; Rook paid for you; salvage logged an empty pod; somebody
   sold the route and she wants a name.
7. **Concourse.** A station going about its day: rationing, a memorial for
   the Aster's crews, warnings about unlicensed lending. Mara: Rook's people
   will come; don't sign anything; show me you can fly.
8. **Test Flight.** Six beacons round Hearth, weapons locked by station
   rules, traffic in the lanes. Rook calls for the first time. On docking,
   Mara: your Kestrel's recorder was wiped on Hearth, but the Aster's
   bridge carried a crash core, still pinging in the field.
9. **Black Box.** Warp to the Aster's grave. Find the core by its signal,
   pull it aboard. Rook's Hollow ships warp in. Fight. The recording: the
   route went out from the engineering console an hour before the attack,
   to a private relay on Hearth, authorised "R. Ayers". Then a pod launch.
   Mara: Rook's crew picked that pod up first. Rook has Renn.

10. **Down the Well.** A call from Earth's surface: Renn ran from Rook and
   is hiding in the quarantined pillar country. Enter the atmosphere, follow
   her markers low through the pillars, under the stone arch and down the
   drowned city's river with Rook's hunter drones behind, climb the
   waterfall where they lose you, land by her stolen Hollow lander. On
   foot, she tells it: she sent the route for her brother's debt, Rook
   promised nobody would be hurt, and she has his ledger. The Hollow are
   Rook's, and the governor of Hearth seals his permits.
