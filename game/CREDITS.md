## Poly Haven

Mara's rocking chair, the village's table and barrels, the tree stumps,
fallen trunks, mossy rocks, ferns and shrubs, and the moon rocks on Ashfall,
the Phantom, the Wanderer and in the Zero-G cave are photo-scanned models
from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0. Decimated for the browser by
art/godot/polyhaven.py. The grass, dandelions and daisies are Poly Haven's
scanned grass_medium_01/02, dandelion_01 and flower_ursinia rendered into
cards (art/godot/grass_cards.py); the pines are built from its scanned
fir_sapling and pine_sapling_small bark (art/godot/pines.py). Hearth's
crater wall is its scanned rock_face_01 and rock_face_02 (stratified
sandstone), with namaqualand_boulder_03 at the foot, on the Zero-G cave's
dome, as Verdance's floating stones and as the root of the crystal
clusters (art/godot/crystals.py, made for the game).

## Quaternius

The people of the Reach are built from Quaternius's Universal Base
Characters, Modular Character Outfits (Fantasy) and Universal Animation
Library (CC0, quaternius.com): heads and hair from the base characters,
peasant and ranger outfits, the animation library's idle, talk, walk, run,
sit and the rest retargeted onto them (art/godot/crew_people.py).
